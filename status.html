Why isn't the Mac agent marked online?

The Mac-side service is intended to communicate with the paired Android device over a direct connection. A public website cannot safely infer whether a particular Mac is powered on, reachable, or paired. The website will only report app-service health if a real, privacy-conscious monitoring source is added.
